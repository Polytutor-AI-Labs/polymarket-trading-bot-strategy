# FAQ

### Does PolyTutor guarantee profit?

No. Prediction-market trading can lose capital. Research, software, and historical examples are not a promise of profit.

### Are these systems currently Production?

No. The public [Strategy Handbook](https://github.com/PolyTutor-Labs/polymarket-strategy-handbook) classifies published pages as Historical / Revalidation Required or External Research / Revalidation Required. Research pages in this repository stay **Research**. Commercial catalog labels (AVAILABLE / CUSTOM BUILD) are delivery labels, not Production or profitability claims.

### Can PolyTutor build my strategy?

Often, after a feasibility review. That review looks at the hypothesis, data, execution path, risk, and whether the idea is specified well enough to engineer. Not every idea should become a bot.

### Can you modify an existing bot?

Yes, after a codebase and architecture review. Work typically focuses on execution, reliability, risk controls, testing, or monitoring.

### Do you provide source code?

It depends on the project agreement and license. This public repository is a showcase, not a source release.

### Can you deploy trading infrastructure?

It depends on the project requirements. Deployment is not implied by a research page.

### Do you provide investment advice?

No. Public material is technical and educational. You remain responsible for trading decisions and compliance.

### Are historical examples guarantees of future performance?

No. Historical, backtested, simulated, paper, or source-reported results do not guarantee future results. Markets, fees, latency, and liquidity change.

### Why aren't exact strategy parameters public?

Exact thresholds, lookbacks, formulas, and production settings are intellectual property, can be unsafe if copied without context, and are not required to understand the research idea. The handbook explains concepts and failure modes. This repository stays at systems and engineering level.
