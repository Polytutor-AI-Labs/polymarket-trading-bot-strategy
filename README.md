### Strategy

**Low-Side Optionality + Paired Hedge**

```text
Cheap Side
   ↓
Position
   ↓
Market Moves
   ↓
Opposite Side Becomes Attractive
   ↓
Potential Hedge
   ↓
Matched Position
```

Research around low-priced outcome exposure and potential two-sided hedge construction when the opposite side later becomes attractive. Combined cost, matched quantity, unmatched exposure, second-leg execution and partial fills all matter. This is not guaranteed arbitrage.

### Strategy

**Intramarket Probability Repricing**

```text
Entry
  ↓
Probability Dislocation
  ↓
Repricing
  ↓
Exit Before Settlement
```

Research around trading probability movement inside short-duration markets rather than necessarily holding every position to settlement. Entry, exit, liquidity, timing and signal expiry are separate problems. This is not a claim that every `@vega7` trade followed one exact algorithm.

Public-profile retrieval was not always consistent during review; market-level records confirm BTC 5-minute participation. Third-party wallet analytics are supporting evidence only.

Full write-up: [vega7-intramarket-repricing.md](./case-studies/trading-accounts/vega7-intramarket-repricing.md)

# Engineering

Trading results depend on more than predicting direction.

> **Direction alone is not an edge. Execution is part of the strategy.**

```text
Market
  ↓
Market Data
  ↓
Signal
  ↓
Strategy
  ↓
Risk
  ↓
Execution
  ↓
Position Management
  ↓
Analytics
```

A trading system has to handle market-data freshness, signal processing, risk validation, order execution, partial fills, leftover exposure, state reconciliation, monitoring and failure recovery. Controls are chosen for the strategy, venue and deployment.

Documented public technical work includes Rust implementations, Polymarket CLOB interaction, Polygon activity used for copy-trading research, and WebSocket market/event streams. Individual deployments differ. This repository does not publish bot source code or proprietary thresholds.
