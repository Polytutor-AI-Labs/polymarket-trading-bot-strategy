## What This Evidence Demonstrates

It demonstrates:

- PolyTutor shows control of the account at the time of recording
- the public account and its activity can be inspected
- screenshots and video are tied to the account being presented

It does **not** demonstrate:

- Polymarket independently verified ownership
- every trade was generated by a bot
- every trade used the documented strategy
- future profitability
- exact private implementation

Ownership proof and strategy analysis serve different purposes. Screenshots may show historical profit/loss. That is historical account activity, not guaranteed performance.

File hashes for the nine published evidence files are in [SHA256SUMS.txt](../../assets/trading-evidence/SHA256SUMS.txt). Hashes identify the published files. They are not independent ownership verification.

## Evidence Chain

Ownership proof shows control and inspectable public activity. Strategy analysis is a separate interpretation of observed patterns and engineering problems.
